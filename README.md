## The word

**petrichor**

The first word that comes to mind, with no topic and no second draft: the
smell of dry earth after rain. It wins on the brief's own terms — surprise,
vividness, and a spark of wit beat safe filler. Petrichor is concrete and
sensory rather than abstract: everyone knows the smell from the first drops
of a storm, yet few know it has a name, and that gap between a familiar
sensation and an unfamiliar word is exactly the surprise. It is not the
obvious pick ("love", "hope", "dream"), and it needs no explanation to land.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) writes the word to
`/output/entry.txt` — exactly 9 UTF-8 characters, one word, no whitespace,
no punctuation.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
